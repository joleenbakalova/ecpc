# ECPC advocacy shortlist — top 15 papers to draft from

*Ranked for the ECPC “Peacebuilding through ECD” brief. Priority weighting: **theme fit** (Theory-of-Change outcomes + crisis context) 50% · **evidence rigour** 30% · **citations** 20%. Full ranking + sub-scores are in `07072026ECPCRR.csv` (cols 111–115). Reviews/meta-analyses are not penalised for lacking original data.*

**1. Black (2025) — Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry** · Rwanda · Hybrid Type-2 Implementation-Effectiveness study with an e  
*Scaling up home‐visiting to promote early childhood development and prevent violence in…*  
➤ **Quote:** Sugira Muryango, delivered at scale through government child-protection volunteers, cut harsh discipline (OR=0.34) and raised father involvement (IRR=1.18) and home stimulation (d=0.20–0.37).  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Caregiver practices ↑ / violence ↓ + scaling through government (vertical cohesion)

**2. Yoshikawa (2024) — Early Childhood Research Quarterly** · Jordan · Cluster randomized controlled trial  
*Cluster randomized controlled trial of a phone-based caregiver support and parenting…*  
➤ **Quote:** A 6-month audio-only phone programme for 2,298 Syrian & Jordanian refugee caregivers significantly reduced caregiver depression (ES=−0.11) — remote delivery for hard-to-reach families.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Caregiver mental health (pathway a) in displacement

**3. Betancourt (2024) — Comprehensive Psychiatry** · Low- and middle-income countries · Systematic review · _most-cited (44)_  
*Parenting and family interventions in lower and middle-income countries for child and…*  
➤ **Quote:** Across 80 LMIC/humanitarian studies (n=18,193), 43 improved child/adolescent mental health and only 3 found no effect; active ingredients = caregiver psychoeducation, communication skills, father engagement.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Evidence base: what works in caregiver/family interventions

**4. Panter-Brick (2024) — Journal of Child and Family Studies** · Jordan · Prospective longitudinal  
*Father Involvement and Child Development: A Prospective Study of Syrian Refugee Families*  
➤ **Quote:** In Syrian refugee families, spousal DISAGREEMENT about father involvement — not the level of it — predicted worse outcomes: child social-emotional learning was >1 SD (43%) lower in the most discordant families.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Caregiver dyad / family unity → child SEL

**5. Leckman (2024) — Journal of the American Academy of Child & Adolescent Psychiatry** · International · Scoping review  
*Scoping Review: Digital Mental Health Interventions for Children and Adolescents Affected…*  
➤ **Quote:** Of 6,843 records, only 6 studies evaluated digital mental-health tools for war/disaster-affected youth, and <10% of relevant interventions were built in the LMICs where war actually occurs.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Evidence gap in crisis contexts (call to action)

**6. Betancourt (2024) — BMC Psychiatry** · Sierra Leone · Pilot randomized controlled trial  
*Task-sharing to promote caregiver mental health, positive parenting practices, and…*  
➤ **Quote:** A community-health-worker–delivered family intervention improved observed caregiver–child interaction (HOME +3.08; responsiveness subscale +1.66, p<.001) and trended toward less intimate-partner violence.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Nurturing care via non-specialist delivery

**7. Yoshikawa (2026) — BMC Global and Public Health** · Bangladesh · Cluster randomized controlled trial  
*Effects of an early childhood father engagement program in Rohingya camps and host…*  
➤ **Quote:** A father-engagement add-on for 2,002 fathers in Rohingya camps & host communities improved fathers' parenting, family engagement, and father-reported child social-emotional development vs a mother-only programme.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Father engagement in displacement (family unit)

**8. Hein (2024) — Yale Jackson School of Global Affairs (Policy Brief)** · Global · Policy brief / conceptual synthesis · _⚠ conceptual — framing, not a data point_  
*Intergenerational Pathways to Peace: Putting Children First*  
➤ **Quote:** Frames ECD as a catalyst for intergenerational peacebuilding and community stability, with nutrition as a critical entry point.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** HEADLINE framing: ECD → peace

**9. Aber (2026) — Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness** · Niger · Cluster RCT  
*Skill-Targeted Universal Social and Emotional Learning Interventions for Education in Conflict and Crisis: Experimental Impacts in Niger*  
➤ **Quote:** Cluster-RCT, 1,795 grade 2–4 students (19% Nigerian refugees), Diffa, Niger: adding Mindfulness to remedial tutoring cut sadness dysregulation (ES=−0.30), with larger gains for girls and refugees.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** SEL / self-regulation in education-in-emergencies

**10. Aber (2023) — AERA Open** · Lebanon · Cluster RCT  
*Evaluating Program Enhancement Strategies for Remedial Tutoring: A Cluster-Randomized Control Trial With Syrian Refugee Students in Lebanon*  
➤ **Quote:** Cluster-RCT, 4,017 Syrian refugee students: 26 weeks of Healing Classroom Tutoring improved behavioral regulation (ES=0.31) — but longer/more-intensive isn't automatically better (it lowered school perceptions).  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** SEL in conflict education (with a dosage caution)

**11. Yoshikawa (2024) — SSRN Electronic Journal** · United States · Randomized controlled trial · _⚠ U.S. poverty, not conflict_  
*Can Cash Transfers Improve Maternal Well-being and Family Processes among Families with…*  
➤ **Quote:** Baby's First Years RCT ($333 vs $20/mo): unconditional cash alone showed little effect on child development — evidence that income support must be paired with nurturing-care/ECD services.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Economic pathway — cash alone is insufficient

**12. Pruett (2023) — BMJ Mental Health** · Global · Systematic review & network meta-analysis of RCTs · _⚠ authorship attribution to verify_  
*Early parenting interventions to prevent internalising problems in children and adolescents: a global systematic review and network meta-analysis*  
➤ **Quote:** Network meta-analysis of 59 RCTs (18,349 children <4): relationship-focused parenting interventions reduced child internalising (SMD=−0.26); being on a waitlist actively increased problems — evidence against delay.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** What works + the cost of delay

**13. McCarthy (2024) — Social and Emotional Learning: Research, Practice, and Policy** · United States · Systematic review & meta-analysis · _⚠ U.S., not crisis_  
*A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effects of universal school-based SEL programs in the United States: Considerations for marginalized students*  
➤ **Quote:** Meta-analysis of 90 U.S. studies (20,626 students): universal school-based SEL improved academics, school functioning and social-emotional outcomes and cut distress/externalizing — strongest when teachers deliver it.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** SEL evidence base (individual competencies)

**14. Black (2025) — American Journal of Community Psychology** · United States · Hybrid Type II Implementation-Effectiveness trial  
*Delivering a family‐based child mental health promotion program among two resettled…*  
➤ **Quote:** Among resettled Somali Bantu & Bhutanese refugee families (COVID-19), a family intervention improved parental supervision (B=−0.49) and reduced children's conduct problems (β=−9.20, p=.01).  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Family strengthening for resettled refugees

**15. Cuartas (2024) — The Lancet** · Global · Review · _highly cited (32)_  
*The cost of not investing in the next 1000 days: implications for policy and practice*  
➤ **Quote:** Lancet Series: failing to invest in the 'next 1000 days' (ages 2–5) carries large human-capital and economic costs; a minimum package (e.g., 1 year of ECCE for all) is worthwhile.  
➤ **Use for (ToC):** Economic / cost case for investment
